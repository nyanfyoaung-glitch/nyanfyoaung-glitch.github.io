Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. About Us

Midnight Leaf Studio produces romantic videos, relationship quotes, and short-form digital content. Our creator-operated application helps manage and publish content to authorized social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

When a social media account is connected, our application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, publishing statuses, and related technical logs. We request only the permissions needed for authorized publishing operations.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, prepare and publish videos, monitor publishing results, prevent duplicate posts, and troubleshoot technical errors.

4. Social Media Platform Integrations

Midnight Leaf Studio uses authorized platform APIs for Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube. TikTok integration is being developed and is subject to TikTok authorization and approval.

YouTube API Services are used for authorized uploads. Use of YouTube-related functionality is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy .

5. Authentication and Security

Authorized credentials are stored locally on the creator's Windows computer using operating-system encryption where implemented. Credentials are not intentionally published on this website. Internet transmissions to platform APIs use secure connections.

6. Information Sharing

Information required for publishing is transmitted to the relevant authorized social media platforms. We do not sell connected-account credentials or personal information to advertisers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account authorization information and publishing records may be retained while an account is connected or while records are required for troubleshooting and duplicate-post protection. Account owners may revoke application access through the applicable social media platform. Locally stored credentials and records can be deleted by the application operator.

8. Website Visitors

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and access logs according to its own privacy practices. This website does not intentionally request visitors' social media passwords.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our publishing system, data practices, or platform requirements.
